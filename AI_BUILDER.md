# Clervo builder authority

This file guides future site, documentation, agent, motion, and product-copy work. It does not authorize implementation or make a production-readiness claim.

## Product story

Lead with **Find → Reason → Execute**:

- Search finds current cited evidence.
- AI reasons across qualified inference supply.
- Sandbox executes bounded code securely.

The product is an outcome platform, not a headline about “three APIs.” Use **Buy outcomes. Not integrations.** Preserve **Install → Ask → Fund → Approve → Result → Receipt**.

Initial Commercial Release pillars are Search, AI, and Sandbox. RPC, Prediction, and Crypto Intelligence are planned post-launch expansion and must never appear callable or available without gate evidence.

## Visual and interaction constraints

Preserve a near-black cinematic canvas, precise white typography, red for incoming request/risk, green for verified result, yellow for degraded state, and prism/beam language. Do not redesign this identity. Motion must clarify the request-to-receipt journey, respect reduced motion, and never simulate proof.

## Content hierarchy

1. Concrete combined outcomes: commerce/price intelligence; real-estate research and monitoring; competitor/market research; lead/company research; document/data processing; developer/data workflows; recurring lawful monitoring and alerts.
2. Exact lifecycle and limitations.
3. Executable guides and one-request demonstrations based on proven routes.
4. Pricing, receipts, provenance, safety, status, and recovery.
5. Planned expansion, clearly separated from launch capabilities.

Never invent prices, providers, commands, screenshots, customers, agents, testimonials, metrics, transactions, or results. Label unfinished behavior `prototype`, `preview`, `planned/post-launch`, `unavailable`, or `placeholder` as applicable.
