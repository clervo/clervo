# Clervo continuous engineering and safety

These rules are always active.

1. Use the master plan as product scope and ordered implementation checklist,
   not as a per-ticket authorization system.
2. Continue automatically through ordinary local tasks, stages, repairs, tests,
   and commits. Do not stop merely because another ticket or stage is next.
3. Working product behavior and current contracts outrank plans and memory.
4. Preserve owner-owned and unrelated uncommitted files.
5. Never expose secrets, spend money, sign payments, or irreversibly mutate
   production/customer/unrelated infrastructure without explicit owner action.
6. Preserve the clean-room legacy boundary, provider terms, payment idempotency,
   sandbox isolation, SSRF protection, cleanup, and cost controls.
7. Record uncertainty honestly and never promote preview, fixture, local, or
   failed behavior into a stronger public claim.
