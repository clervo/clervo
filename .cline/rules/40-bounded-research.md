# Bounded research and provider-candidate handling

Research exists to answer one ticket decision, not to expand scope.

- Use primary and official sources.
- Set a time limit and candidate limit before research.
- For ordinary adapter/provider selection: maximum 30 minutes and 5 serious candidates unless the ticket says otherwise.
- Record the decision and rejected alternatives briefly.
- Do not install or combine all trending repositories.
- A repository may be:
  - selected for the current bounded role;
  - deferred candidate;
  - blocked by terms/security;
  - rejected.
- Popularity, stars, or “trending” status do not prove production suitability.
- Public shared service instances are not production supply unless explicitly qualified and permitted.
- Open-source software can still have infrastructure, maintenance, upstream-engine, legal, and operational costs.
- No candidate becomes a core dependency without a contract, tests, failure behavior, and a documented replacement path.
