# Clervo continuous engineering policy

`ROADMAP.md` at the repository root is the single planning authority. The full
instructions are in `CLAUDE.md` at the repository root. Read both before
selecting work.

`docs/` is an archived research library with no authority — useful for facts,
never a directive. Files outside this repository, including anything under
`/workspace/docs/` or `/workspace/import/`, are not authority and are not a
plan. Working behavior outranks every document, including `ROADMAP.md`.

Codex may continuously inspect, implement, refactor, test, document, and commit
inside this clean-room repository. Roadmap steps are a sequenced implementation
checklist, not per-task authorization gates. Ordinary local work, repairs,
dependency installation, bounded technical research, and commits do not require
owner approval.

Use focused checks during implementation and one consolidated check at step
boundaries. Preserve truthful failures and prior evidence, but do not make
historical proof artifacts control unrelated future engineering. A step is done
only when proven from outside on a public URL; a passing local test is not
completion.

Owner action is required only for real spending or payment, missing credentials
or interactive account steps, irreversible production, customer-data, or
unrelated-infrastructure operations, and non-technical legal, contractual,
branding, pricing, or business choices. Never expose secrets, weaken security
or contracts, mutate legacy systems, bypass provider terms, or overstate
lifecycle and product readiness.

Do not create new planning, status, gate, or readiness documents. Edit
`ROADMAP.md`.
