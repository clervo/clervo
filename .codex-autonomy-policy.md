# Clervo continuous engineering policy

Codex may continuously inspect, implement, refactor, test, document, and commit
inside this clean-room repository. Roadmap stages and ticket identifiers are a
sequenced implementation checklist, not per-task authorization gates. Ordinary
local work, repairs, dependency installation, bounded technical research, and
commits do not require owner approval or a dispatcher cycle.

Use focused checks during implementation and one consolidated check at full
stage boundaries. Preserve truthful failures and prior evidence, but do not
make historical proof artifacts control unrelated future engineering.

Owner action is required only for real spending or payment, missing credentials
or interactive account steps, irreversible production/customer-data or
unrelated-infrastructure operations, and non-technical legal, contractual,
branding, pricing, or business choices. Never expose secrets, weaken security or
contracts, mutate legacy systems, bypass provider terms, or overstate lifecycle
and product readiness.
