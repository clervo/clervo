## Proven decision

1. Brave is optional and cannot gate ready search supply.
2. A selected metasearch broker must be self-hosted.
3. The broker must have at least two separately named, independently qualified upstream providers and failure domains.
4. A public shared SearXNG instance is never production supply.
5. Common Crawl must be directly qualified as a separate archive/corpus failure domain.
6. At most one extraction worker may be evaluated/selected behind `bounded_retrieval_adapter`.
7. A selected worker fails closed unless deterministic fixtures, timeout enforcement, resource limits, and failure isolation all pass.
8. Deferred candidates cannot be core dependencies.
9. Provider, source, and worker substitution policy is exact identity only.

## Current qualification truth

- SearXNG: architecture candidate only; not installed and no upstream engine is live-qualified.
- Common Crawl: required independent path; no new live qualification was performed in N4.19.
- Brave Search API: optional adapter, not required, not live-qualified.
- Crawl4AI: sole evaluated worker, unselected and `not_run` against Clervo-specific safety proofs.
- ScrapeGraphAI, Agent Browser, browser-use-style tools, Agent Reach: deferred and uninstalled.
