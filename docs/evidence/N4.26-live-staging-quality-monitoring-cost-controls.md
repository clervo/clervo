default-deny, non-root, read-only, ephemeral, resource-limited execution
boundary. It does not prove runtime isolation. Attempt one was denied by the

during the large pinned-image pull. No Crawl4AI/Playwright runtime attestation,

search read-through; removal and denylist invalidation remained repository-only.
