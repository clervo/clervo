## Page inventory

The single route inventory generated 153 prerendered content routes plus a
real 404 document:

- 35 fixed pages/templates, including home, Start, Product, six family pages,
  Catalog, Pricing, Docs, Status, Proof, Proof Lab, Security, Trust, and 404.
- 29 canonical operation pages generated from the live/catalog authority.
- 89 model pages generated from the canonical AI catalog (85 canonical model
  identities and four Clervo routing aliases).

Every model page has a distinct title, description, canonical URL, OpenGraph
metadata, JSON-LD, exact Clervo ID, capabilities, availability/health,
sellability, pricing behavior, and a tested B11 client example.
