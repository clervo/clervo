# N4.27S frozen-repair staging qualification and blocked decision

N4.27S completed its once-only private-staging qualification and failed the
mandatory product gate. The result is preserved without tuning or rerun.
Search is not commercially competitive, production-qualified, an Exa-parity
result, the best Web search, or the Stage 4 reference pattern. N4.28 and Stage
5 remain unauthorized.

## Browser, safety and hostile content

Browser qualification failed: 4/20 startups and JavaScript runs succeeded,
although all 20 teardown checks were clean, successful-run p95 was 2753.65 ms,
and final retained-state/orphan counts were zero. All eight hostile-page runs
failed before extraction, so the prompt-injection boundary did not obtain the
required 100% live proof.

Independently exercised gateway/security controls passed private, loopback,
link-local and metadata denial; target, redirect and connected-address checks;
controlled DNS rebinding; robots policy; MIME, decompression and output limits;
gateway-only containment; read-only/non-root/resource limits; and stopped-
runtime honesty. No secret, wallet, query or customer payload was observed in
captured monitoring.

## Cache, locale, operations and latency

The repeatable operations suite passed every recorded gate: connected-cache
failure honesty, miss/fresh/stale/revalidation, route isolation, integrity
rejection, eviction/removal/denylist invalidation, source circuits, half-open
restoration, route concurrency, bounded timeout storm, operation quota,
all-routes-down, global stop/restoration and unbounded-cost stop.

Network-inclusive three-run medians were 1142.898 ms commerce, 1206.815 ms
property, 1016.555 ms company, 1415.696 ms research and 381.375 ms developer.
The captured aggregate process telemetry reported p50 732.228 ms, p95 1622.769
ms and p99 1636.533 ms. Source/ranking/cache timing and per-scenario variance
are preserved in the hash-bound operations artifact.

Locale was propagated to every source. Wikimedia selected the requested
language subdomain; all source limitations were explicitly disclosed as
unsupported rather than silently accepted. Results expose `fetchedAt`,
`observedAt`, `expiresAt`, freshness/cache state, route and fresh-retrieval
failure. Combined citations bind exact URLs, extraction identities, hashes,
offsets and quote slices and scored 1.0000 validity.
