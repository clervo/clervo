# Clervo Platform solution-pack and use-case framework

This is a ranked product backlog, not implemented inventory. The master plan
defines product scope and ordering; this file does not claim that backlog items
are implemented or ready.

## Revenue model

The all-six Clervo Platform can earn at five layers without changing stable
capability contracts:

1. metered machine-readable API/x402 calls;
2. bounded comparison and evidence bundles;
3. recurring monitoring, changes, and alerts;
4. customer-authorized private source/index configuration; and
5. managed recurring intelligence workflows.

Every layer exposes exact capability identities, connector access modes,
maximum charge, provenance, citations, freshness, verification, failure state,
and receipt. The First Revenue Release requires stable Live
Intelligence/Search, AI, Secure Sandbox, RPC, Prediction, and Crypto
Intelligence cores; combined workflows; a cross-pillar contract freeze; one
shared MCP, TypeScript SDK, Python SDK, raw HTTP, onboarding, design,
documentation, discovery, SEO, and distribution pass; production deployment;
real demonstrations; and one useful external paid result.

## Stable solution packs

### 1. Commerce and Marketplace Intelligence

- **Jobs:** product and price scan, stock/promotion watch, seller comparison,
  listing deduplication, catalog enrichment, and new/removed listing changes.
- **Sources:** `open_web`, `official_api`, BYOC, partner, user-authorized, or
  customer-supplied sources only.
- **Launch proof:** same-product recall, variant/field accuracy, duplicates,
  freshness, changes, citations, cost ceiling, and safe unsupported-platform
  behavior.
- **Boundary:** Facebook Marketplace and other closed marketplaces are not
  unrestricted anonymous Web sources.

### 2. Property and Local-Market Intelligence

- **Jobs:** criteria-matched listings, price changes, duplicate properties,
  comparable inventory, neighborhoods, agencies, and local supply maps.
- **Sources:** permitted public/official listings, feeds, business sites,
  directories, and customer-authorized data.
- **Launch proof:** location/field accuracy, duplicate grouping, new/reduced
  change detection, source coverage, citations, safe language, and cost.
- **Boundary:** no gated scraping, discrimination-sensitive ranking, personal
  dossiers, valuation guarantee, or autonomous high-impact decision.

### 3. Company and Competitive Intelligence

- **Jobs:** competitor change radar, pricing/product/job changes,
  announcements, partnerships, account evidence, and public business signals.
- **Sources:** company sites, official records, feeds, documentation, and
  permitted news/public sources.
- **Launch proof:** official-source ranking, entity disambiguation, field and
  change accuracy, conflicting-source visibility, citations, and cost.
- **Boundary:** no sensitive-person harvesting, data-broker enrichment, or
  unsolicited outreach execution.

### 4. Research and Evidence

- **Jobs:** company evidence packs, official-source research, public records,
  cited datasets, documentation, policy watches, and evidence bundles.
- **Sources:** open, official, authorized, and customer-supplied sources.
- **Launch proof:** recall, precision, source diversity, exact citation
  validity, freshness, contradictions, completeness, safety, and cost.
- **Boundary:** no paywall/access bypass, fabricated facts, legal conclusion,
  or autonomous regulated decision.

### 5. Developer and Agent Context

- **Jobs:** current documentation retrieval, safe fetch/extract, version-aware
  deduplicated context, release/advisory watches, and agent-ready evidence.
- **Sources:** official docs, public repositories, feeds, advisories, and
  authorized customer material.
- **Launch proof:** version/date sensitivity, code-block preservation,
  duplicates, citations, malicious-content isolation, degradation, and cost.
- **Boundary:** no unauthorized private repository access, exploit execution,
  secret ingestion, or invented SDK/package availability.

## Priority demonstrations

1. competitor website change radar;
2. product and price scan;
3. property listing watch;
4. company evidence pack;
5. local market comparison; and
6. current technical-documentation context.

Each demonstration must be real and reproducible, show actual sources and
limitations, and include the exact result schema, quote, receipt, freshness,
degradation, and replay behavior when those layers are proven.

## Integrated product evolution

The permanent narrative is **Find → Understand → Act**:

- current Live Intelligence/Search `preview`: **Discover → Retrieve →
  Structure → Verify → Monitor**;
- unavailable AI core: **Understand → Analyze → Score → Explain → Report**;
- unavailable Secure Sandbox core: **Transform → Execute → Automate →
  Export**; and
- unavailable RPC, Prediction, and Crypto Intelligence cores.

All six are First Revenue Release prerequisites. Build and stabilize their
product cores privately in roadmap order without altering stable
capability identifiers. After the Stage 12 core and cross-pillar contract
freeze, Stage 13 performs one shared API, MCP, SDK, onboarding, design,
documentation, discovery, JSON-LD, sitemap, SEO, and distribution pass.

Only Search is currently a `preview`; the other five cores are `unavailable`
and have not started implementation. Stage 4 passed its evidence-bound exit.
N5.1 established the unfrozen six-pillar registry and N5.2 implemented
deterministic evidence comparison and typed changes. Monitoring execution,
delivery, and alerts remain unproven.

## Promotion rule

A use case becomes sellable only after its qualification proves lawful source
access, a stable useful-result contract, controlled fixtures, commercial
benchmark, bounded x402 behavior, production operations, monitoring, recovery,
real demonstration, and external paid value. Missing proof remains visible.
