# N5.2 — Live Intelligence evidence comparison and change detection

## Ticket

- **Stage:** 5 — Live Intelligence productization and platform-registry foundation.
- **One question:** Can two independently produced Search evidence snapshots be compared deterministically without confusing refresh timestamps, result IDs, or array order with material evidence change?
- **Inputs allowed:** N5.1 internal registry; proven Stage 4 `SearchResponse`, canonical URL, citation verification, JCS/SHA-256, and provenance contracts. Frozen/final Stage 4 evidence is read-only and is not a fixture source.
- **Files allowed to change:** N5.2 contract TypeScript/export, strict schemas/fixtures, internal schema-visibility manifest, canonical registry operation, focused tests/package script, this ticket, active state/dispatcher, append-only journal, and external master-plan handoff.
- **Freshness date:** 2026-08-01.
- **Secrets involved:** none.

## Preflight and decision

RFC 8785 remains the deterministic hashing basis. RFC 6902 supplies clear add/remove/replace semantics, but emitting raw JSON Patch values would duplicate untrusted evidence and make array order noisy. N5.2 therefore emits a bounded typed change report keyed by canonical URL, with JCS hashes instead of copied content. W3C PROV's entity/activity distinction informs the separation between a snapshot identity and the comparison activity, while existing Clervo provenance remains authoritative.

Comparison identity binds exact query, language, and region. Baseline must precede current. Material fields are title, snippet, evidence text, publication time, source identity, authority/relevance score, and rank; retrieval time and run-local result IDs are observation metadata and do not alone create a change. Events are deterministically sorted and classified as `added`, `removed`, or `modified`; `modified` names exact changed fields. Inputs are revalidated for canonical URLs, unique entities, timestamps, ranks, and exact citations.
