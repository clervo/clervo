# NPLAN.2 — Clervo Live Intelligence First Revenue Release authority

- **Stage:** planning amendment while Stage 4 remains active
- **Question:** Can Clervo launch one complete, revenue-capable Live
  Intelligence product after Search is proven, then add AI, Sandbox, and later
  pillars without rebranding the company, breaking stable interfaces,
  weakening truthful claims, or inheriting the legacy runtime?
- **Decision:** yes. Clervo remains outcome infrastructure for agents; Clervo
  Live Intelligence becomes the First Revenue Release; AI, Sandbox, RPC,
  Prediction, and Crypto Intelligence are ordered additive expansions.
- **Inputs allowed:** owner launch-authority intake, master plan, N4.25 evidence,
  active lifecycle/discovery/release/builder/brand/onboarding/marketing/website/
  documentation authorities, historical records as read-only evidence.
- **Files allowed:** the exact authority, release-scope, discovery, tests,
  generated artifacts, ticket/evidence/journal/handoff, and master-plan files
  affected by this decision.
- **Prohibited:** N4.26 implementation; runtime search changes; cloud,
  credential, provider, deployment, payment, wallet, USDC, legacy, AI,
  Sandbox, RPC, Prediction, or Crypto mutation; canonical `npm test`.
- **Cost/network:** repository-local only; zero external calls, zero provider or
  infrastructure cost, 0 USDC.
