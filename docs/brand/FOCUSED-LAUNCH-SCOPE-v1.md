# Clervo Shop-Open brand scope

- Category: **Outcome infrastructure for agents**
- Promise: **Give your agent a task. Get a verified result**
- Commercial promise: **Buy outcomes. Not integrations**
- Narrative: **Find → Understand → Act**
- Active offer: **Live Web Search**
- Buyer value: useful structured results, citations, simple machine access,
  wallet-native payment, receipt, and safe replay

The six permanent families remain visible as the platform, but public CTAs and
availability must match generated truth.

Use confident outcome language. Do not expose gate terminology, stale lifecycle
claims, provider qualification language, fake proof, or unsupported superiority
claims.

Preserve the approved visual authority without copying its historical product
snapshot.
