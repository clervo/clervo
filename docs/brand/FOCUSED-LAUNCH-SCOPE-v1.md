# Clervo brand and motion scope addendum v1

This Markdown addendum is the editable authority for the focused launch scope. No binary brand or motion asset was found or overwritten.

## Unchanged system

- Tagline: **Buy outcomes. Not integrations.**
- Journey: **Install → Ask → Fund → Approve → Result → Receipt**.
- Visual identity: near-black cinematic canvas, precise white typography, red incoming request/risk, green verified result, yellow degraded state, and prism/beam language.
- Proof rule: no fake product behavior, availability, price, provider, customer, agent, transaction, metric, screenshot, or demonstration.

## Updated product sentence

> One wallet and one machine-discoverable x402 platform to find with Search, reason with AI, and execute in a secure Sandbox.

## Capability hierarchy

1. Initial Commercial Release: Search, AI, Sandbox.
2. Combined promise: **Find → Reason → Execute**.
3. Planned post-launch expansion: RPC, Prediction, Crypto Intelligence.

Launch content emphasizes real agents, executable guides, concrete customer outcomes, bounded approval, useful results, and receipts. It must not imply six-service availability. Planned expansion may appear only as roadmap context with an explicit planned/post-launch label.

## Motion narrative

The red request beam enters; Search resolves evidence; AI refracts evidence into reasoning; Sandbox executes the bounded job; verified green output exits with a receipt. Yellow exposes degradation without hiding it. Future expansion beams remain visually inactive and labeled planned until qualified.
