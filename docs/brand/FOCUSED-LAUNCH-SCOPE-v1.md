# Clervo brand and focused commercial scope

## Locked messaging

- **Company:** Clervo
- **Human promise:** Give your agent a task. Get a verified result.
- **Commercial promise:** Buy outcomes. Not integrations.
- **Technical category:** Outcome infrastructure for AI agents.
- **Mechanism:** Find → Understand → Act.
- **Operating model:** Acquire → Operate → Prove.

Do not combine the company name and technical category into an invented tagline.

## Product hierarchy

- Parent product: Clervo Platform
- Installation/runtime wedge: ClervoRouter
- Permanent families:
  1. Search
  2. AI
  3. Secure Sandbox
  4. Multi-chain RPC
  5. Prediction
  6. Crypto Intelligence

A focused commercial workstream may promote the first operation that is
genuinely sellable. It may not redefine Clervo as a one-product company, hide
the permanent platform, or present unavailable operations as callable.

## Visual authority

Preserve the locked v4 visual program, Hollow Apex identity, Solid Apex Core,
semantic state language, high-end motion, responsive composition, reduced
motion, accessibility, and DOM-owned proof and recovery.

Website fixtures establish experience structure, not product truth.

## Proof rule

Never invent or imply availability, price, provider, customer, transaction,
revenue, demand, benchmark, receipt, screenshot, incident, or result.

Every customer-facing claim must be operation-level and traceable to the
canonical registry and current evidence.
