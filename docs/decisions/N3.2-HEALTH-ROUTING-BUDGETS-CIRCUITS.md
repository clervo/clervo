# N3.2 health, routing, budget, and circuit decision

Date: 2026-07-30

## Decision

Clervo routes only through deterministic, repository-local gates. A candidate must exactly support the requested product, hold current passing qualification, have fresh routable health evidence, expose a non-expired maximum supplier-cost estimate, have circuit capacity, fit the operation deadline, and obtain a local cost-and-request budget reservation before execution may begin.

Circuit breakers use `closed`, `open`, and `half_open` states. A bounded rolling failure threshold opens the circuit, a cooldown prevents repeated traffic, and only one half-open probe may be in flight. A successful probe closes the circuit; a failed probe immediately reopens it. Provider `unknown` health, stale health, missing required dependency evidence, unavailable database/network/facilitator dependencies, and unknown execution outcomes fail closed.

Budget information is not advisory. A unique atomic reservation consumes both supplier-cost capacity and request quota before a route is selected. Settlement moves held capacity to spent capacity; release returns it; neither transition may occur twice. This ticket proves deterministic in-memory invariants only, not multi-process atomicity or durable enforcement.

## Bounded preflight evidence

- Microsoft Azure Architecture Center, Circuit Breaker pattern, updated 2025-03-21 and reviewed 2026-07-30: circuit breaking prevents repeated calls likely to fail, is distinct from retry, uses closed/open/half-open states, and permits limited trial requests after a timeout before closing or reopening.
- Repository-local adapter contract: error classification already distinguishes safe pre-consumption retry from reconciliation and records whether a provider may have consumed the request.
- Repository-local commerce contract: atomic asset amounts and explicit execution-unknown quarantine are retained; routing never guesses that an unknown provider outcome is safe to repeat.

## Explicit exclusions

- No live provider, facilitator, database, queue, network, payment, wallet, cloud, IAM, deployment, or traffic mutation is performed.
- No upstream rate-limit header is accepted as execution authorization.
- No PostgreSQL transaction, distributed lock, multi-process reservation, process-crash recovery, or durable circuit persistence is claimed.
- No automatic retry occurs after an unknown or possibly consumed provider outcome.

## Rejected alternatives

- Route on health status without freshness: rejected because stale success evidence can conceal an outage.
- Check budgets after provider execution: rejected because a hard switch must prevent traffic, not merely report overspend.
- Permit multiple half-open probes: rejected because concurrent probes can recreate the overload the circuit is intended to stop.
- Fail over on timeouts regardless of consumption: rejected because it can duplicate supplier work and cost.
