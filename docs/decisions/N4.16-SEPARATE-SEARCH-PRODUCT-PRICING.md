# N4.16 Separate Search Product Pricing

## Decision

`search.query` remains the stable HTTP operation name and the two existing endpoints remain unchanged. Billable product identity is separate from operation identity:

- `synthesize=false` selects `search.web`, priced at 1000 atomic mock-USDC units under `search-web-mock-1`.
- `synthesize=true` selects `search.answer`, priced at 2500 atomic mock-USDC units under `search-answer-mock-1`.

The normalized synthesis flag deterministically selects the product. Product identity participates in the canonical route-bound request hash and is then carried into executor input, HTTP output, quote, payment challenge, ledger charge, immutable receipt, discovery, catalog, and `llms.txt`. A synthesis product must return a synthesis report; raw retrieval may not be relabeled as synthesis.

## Replay and payment binding

Quotes remain sealed over product ID, request hash, price version, and maximum charge. Mock payment verification requires exact quote hash, quote ID, request hash, and amount equality. Consequently, a raw quote or payment cannot authorize synthesis, and an idempotency key completed for one product conflicts with the other product rather than replaying stored output or charge.

## Scope

The published amounts are deterministic repository-local mock prices used to prove product and commerce binding. The default paid route remains explicitly non-payable, real x402 verification and settlement are not implemented, and no live supplier cost or staging willingness-to-pay evidence is claimed.

## Rejected alternatives

- Add a synthesis surcharge to one generic product: rejected because the exit criterion requires separately published raw and synthesis prices and exact product identity is safer for receipts and replay.
- Infer product identity only while constructing a quote: rejected because free results, executor boundaries, idempotency, and receipts would remain ambiguous.
- Add new raw and answer HTTP paths: rejected because the existing strict request mode already selects behavior and no route proliferation is needed to establish exact product identity.
